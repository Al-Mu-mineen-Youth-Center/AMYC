Who We Are

Al-Mu'mineen Youth Center (AMYC) is a non-profit organization committed to supporting at-risk youth and new immigrants as they navigate their journey in America. We understand the unique challenges faced by young people adapting to a new culture, language, and educational system.

Since our founding, we have served hundreds of families, providing educational resources, mentorship, and community support. Our programs are designed to bridge cultural gaps, enhance academic performance, and foster a sense of belonging.

Our Initiatives

Comprehensive programs for holistic development

📚

Academic Support

Tutoring services, homework assistance, and educational workshops to help students excel in their studies and adapt to the American education system.

🗣️

Language Programs

English as a Second Language (ESL) classes for youth and families, helping to break down language barriers and improve communication skills.

👥

Mentorship

One-on-one mentorship connecting at-risk youth with caring adults who provide guidance, support, and positive role models.

💼

Career Exposure

Job readiness training, resume building, interview preparation, and exposure to a wide variety of job fields to help young people prepare for their professional future.

🎨

Cultural Programs

Activities that celebrate cultural heritage while fostering integration, including art, music, and community events.

🎓

Islamic Education

Lessons delivered through a traditional teaching setting to enhance proficiency in personal and communal religious practice
